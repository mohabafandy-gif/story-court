Story Court

Story Court is a short-video channel on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. Each episode retells an everyday dilemma (a family argument, a workplace conflict, a neighbour dispute) in our own words, then asks viewers to deliver the verdict in the comments.

About this app

"Story Court" is also the name of the private tool the channel owner uses to publish the channel's own videos. It signs in only to the owner's own YouTube channel, uploads the channel's videos, posts a comment on them, and reads the channel's own view statistics. It is not offered to other users.

Stories are inspired by public posts and fully rewritten; names and details are changed. Narration uses an AI voice.
