Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers the Story Court publishing app (the "App"), a private tool used only by the owner of the Story Court channel.

What the App accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the App uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to: upload videos to the owner's own channel, post comments on those videos, and read view, like, comment and subscriber statistics for the owner's own videos.

What the App does not do

The App does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or any other users. It does not sell data, show ads, or access any account other than the owner's.

Storage

Access tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared with third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
